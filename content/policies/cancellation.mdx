This policy explains how to cancel or reschedule meetings, projects and course enrollments with AIDMBD International Ltd ("we", "us").

## Meetings

A meeting request isn't a booking until we confirm a time by email.

- **To reschedule**, reply to our confirmation email and we'll find another time.
- **If you can't attend**, please let us know at least 24 hours before the meeting, so we can offer the time to someone else.
- **If we need to move a meeting**, we'll tell you as early as we can and suggest new times.

Any meeting fee, and what happens to it if a meeting is cancelled, is agreed with you before the meeting.

## Projects

Every project starts with a written proposal. It sets out:

- The scope, deliverables and timeline
- The payment schedule
- What happens if either side cancels, including payment for work already completed and handover of that work

Please read these terms before you approve a proposal, and ask us about anything that isn't clear.

## Monthly support and services

Ongoing services, such as maintenance plans or virtual assistant support, have a notice period stated in your agreement. To cancel, contact us in writing and give that notice.

## Course enrollments

If you need to cancel a course enrollment, the refund terms on the course page apply. See our [Refund Policy](/policies/refund).

If we have to reschedule or cancel a live batch, we'll contact you directly with the options available to you.

## How to cancel

[Send us a message](/contact), or reply to the email thread for your meeting or project. We'll confirm the cancellation by email.
