This policy explains what personal information AIDMBD International Ltd ("we", "us") collects through this website, why we collect it and what you can ask us to do with it.

## What we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us through the forms on this website:

- **Meeting requests** ([Book a Consultation](/book)): your name and email, the meeting type, the service or course you're interested in, your preferred date, time and timezone, and your message. Depending on the meeting type, you can also add a phone or WhatsApp number, company or institution, website, budget range, expected timeline or skill level.
- **Course waitlists and enrollment requests**: your name, email and preferred course format.
- **Contact messages**: your name, email, the topic and your message.
- **Talent pool applications** ([Careers](/careers)): your name, email, the area you're interested in, an optional portfolio or profile link and your message.

We don't ask for payment details, identity documents or passwords on this website.

## Why we use it

We use the information you send to:

- Reply to you and arrange any meeting you've requested
- Tell you when a course you're waiting for opens for enrollment
- Prepare a proposal or quotation if you ask for one
- Consider you for current and future roles, if you've joined the talent pool

We don't sell your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who else handles it

When you submit a form, your details are sent to our team by email through Resend, an email delivery provider. The website is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which processes basic technical information such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the site secure. These providers handle your information only to provide their service to us.

## Cookies and analytics

This website doesn't currently use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, we'll update this policy first.

## How long we keep it

We keep your details for as long as we need them to respond to you and to manage any work, enrollment or application that follows. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

## Your choices

You can ask us to:

- Tell you what information we hold about you
- Correct anything that's wrong
- Delete your information
- Stop contacting you

To make a request, [send us a message](/contact). We'll reply by email.

## Changes to this policy

We'll update this page when our practices change, and the date at the top of the page will show when it was last revised.
