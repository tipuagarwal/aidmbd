This policy explains how refunds work for courses and services from AIDMBD International Ltd ("we", "us").

## Courses

Courses currently open by waitlist, and we don't take any payment through this website. Joining a waitlist or sending an enrollment request is free.

When a course opens for enrollment, its page will state, before you pay:

- The course fee, including the currency
- How long you keep access to the course
- Whether and when a refund is available, for both live and recorded formats

The refund terms shown on the course page when you enroll are the ones that apply to your enrollment.

## Services

For services, the refund terms are part of your written proposal, alongside the deliverables, payment schedule and support period. Please read them before you approve the proposal.

## Third-party costs

Some costs are paid to other companies rather than to us, such as advertising budgets, tool or API subscriptions, hosting and domain names. We always show these separately from our fees. Refunds for them depend on the provider's own terms, not on this policy.

## How to ask for a refund

[Send us a message](/contact) with your name, the course or project, and the reason for your request. We'll reply by email and explain the next steps.
